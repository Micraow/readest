native source replay passed, but the region-order gate failed: five protected
image leaves occupy the same horizontal band and their gutters do not meet the
existing safe-column-cut threshold. The recorded ten pair conflicts denote
vertical-band overlap, not shared two-dimensional source ink. The system refused
to guess an order and stopped before generating a reader. Source-PDF fallback
was generated. No retry or resumed stages were counted as success.

two-row photographic transformation grid, plus a colored matrix graphic,
captions and inline notation. Independent source-support asset generation,
selection mapping, responsive reader validation and reader visual acceptance
were not reached; they are **not passed**. Browser acceptance remains unverified.

rather than choosing an internal semantic order. It must preserve every source
